Roth$child — born Rothschild J. Toussaint — builds worlds out of sound, word and image. Mawon Freedom Arts is where they live.

Hailing from the 509 (Haiti) and the 305 (Miami), Roth$child creates worlds with lyrical brevity and dynamic instrumentalism. A rap aficionado and historian, he sees his talent as omnipresent in the genre.

Fixated on being versatile, he infuses a plethora of genres into his music and lets his mind wander — creating worlds within sonic measures.

Why mawon

In Haitian Kreyòl, mawon names the maroons — the people who freed themselves from slavery and built their own communities in the mountains.

In Port-au-Prince, Nèg Mawon — the Unknown Maroon — blows a conch shell, a broken chain at his ankle, calling people to freedom. Mawon Freedom Arts takes its name from that call: make the work on your own terms, and make enough noise that others can find their way to it.
